# Bourse Chrome extension: privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

The Bourse extension (Bourse: Congress & Insider Trades on Stock Pages) is published by Intelekt Cars Sp. z o.o., ul. Pszczela 8, 03-112 Warsaw, Poland (KRS 0001198793, NIP 5243052904). Contact: bourse.official@gmail.com.

## What the extension stores

Only the Bourse API key you paste into it. The key is saved with Chrome storage in your Chrome profile.

## What the extension sends

When you open a stock quote page on a supported site, the extension reads the ticker from the page address and sends that ticker and your API key to api.bourse.pl to get trade data for that one ticker. Nothing else from the page is read or sent.

## What the extension does not do

It does not collect browsing history, page content, personal information, location or analytics data. It does not use cookies or trackers, and it does not sell or share any data.

## Data on our servers

api.bourse.pl counts requests per API key to apply plan limits. The Bourse privacy policy at https://www.bourse.pl/legal/privacy/ describes how account data is handled.

## Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes the stored key. You can also delete or replace your API key in the Bourse app.
